Interactive Posts

as a casual user, I want interactive posts to show me a result or product

	1) easy to understand UI for me to click on
	2) reachable goals without adding extra effort

as a game developer, interactive posts allow me to create mini games within the post for user to engage with
	
	1) customization ability to show my vision to other users
	2)  each post should show me the amount of users who interacted with it

as a data researcher, Interactive posts will allow me to collect data with the public due to instagrams wide spread
	1) listing of options for me to create mini surverys for users
	2) collecting the data from each post to then user research

as a child, interactive posts would be a fun way for me to learn information while using social media
	1) ability to easily interact with these posts and use them for educational purposes
	2) to gain a reward from interacting with the post 

as a customer, interactive posts would allow me to see the options available from a seller without calling

	1) the ability to see what options are abilable for me to choose from in a product or from a seller
	2) the ability to customize said product to my liking with said options
